## About me

I'm an architectural designer focused on **data center planning, BIM, and architectural visualization**. I work across concept design, existing-building conversions, coordinated drawings, and technical presentations.

My experience includes **120+ rack positions across six data center projects**, from a 20 ft modular container to a 48-rack technical level. Alongside architecture, I direct AI-assisted research and digital product workflows, checking outputs against drawings, sources, and real dimensions.

- Architectural, Data Center & Digital Designer at **Padisar Informatics**
- Studying Architecture at **Islamic Azad University, Central Tehran Branch**
- Former instructor of **Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, and Adobe XD**
- Persian: native · English: upper-intermediate
